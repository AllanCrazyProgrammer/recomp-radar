## classic-click.wav

Middle Mouse Click by 1j01:
https://opengameart.org/content/middle-mouse-click

Sources:
- https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/middle-click-press.wav
- https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/middle-click-release.wav

Adaptation: trimmed the recorded press/release, combined with a 25 ms gap,
mixed to mono, normalized and applied 1 ms edge fades. No Windows system
audio is included; this is a real mouse recording for the classic click.

## mechanical-key-medium.wav / mechanical-key-hard.wav

Mechanical key medium.wav and mechanical key hard.wav by bigmonmulgrew:
- https://freesound.org/people/bigmonmulgrew/sounds/378084/
- https://freesound.org/people/bigmonmulgrew/sounds/378085/

Downloaded publicly available high-quality previews from Freesound:
- https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/378/378084_6260145-hq.mp3
- https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/378/378085_6260145-hq.mp3

Adaptation: decoded to 16-bit PCM WAV, trimmed the silence around each
recorded stroke, mixed to mono, normalized and applied 1 ms edge fades.
The application alternates these two recordings across four playback voices.
